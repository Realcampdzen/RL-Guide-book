## Typography pairing (default)

- Display serif: **Cormorant Garamond**
- Body/UI sans: **Source Sans 3**

Notes:
- Headlines: tighter leading, short lines, avoid long ALL CAPS.
- Body: keep line length comfortable and add whitespace.

## Color direction (semantic)

Suggested semantic roles:

- Background: parchment/linen warm neutral
- Foreground: ink/charcoal
- Accent: antique brass (sparingly)
- Secondary accent: oxblood / deep walnut (sparingly)

Guideline: prefer **warm** grays and borders; keep contrast high for body text.

## Imagery direction

- Warm studio light, shallow depth of field for detail shots.
- Show joinery, carving, finishing.
- Keep consistent grading; avoid mixed temperatures on one page.
